# Product

## Register
product

## Users
A roofing company owner managing 1–10 canvassers, and canvassers working outdoors on iPhone and Android. The owner reviews accountability, field hours, territory coverage, and homeowner leads. Reps record doors quickly between conversations and need to continue working with poor cell service.

## Product Purpose
Tie shifts, routes, individual door visits, and leads to a single field workflow. Territories are drawn and assigned by the owner. Reps add houses as they go. Inspection requests go to the office for confirmation through JobNimbus. Success means preserved field records, clear GPS exceptions, and a useful owner overview.

## Confirmed Decisions
The approved plan establishes a practical operational product, high-contrast outdoor-friendly screens, text and icons beside outcome colors, owner review of questionable GPS records, and explicit offline and tracking-gap states. These decisions guide the interface without requiring a separate branding exercise.

## Design Principles
Fast door recording. Put territory context first. Show the age and quality of location data. Preserve history and make corrections visible. Separate sample data from connected operations. Do not imply that GPS proves a knock occurred.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large touch targets, readable outdoor contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic web controls, and text labels for all map outcomes. Honor reduced motion.
